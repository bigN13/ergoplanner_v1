# UK Water Industry Standards Symbols PRD

## Overview
Implementation of UK water industry specific symbols and standards for Ergoplanner.

## Requirements

### FR6.3: UK Water Industry Standards
**Priority:** P0 (Critical)
**Description:** UK water company standard symbols

#### Thames Water Standards
- Treatment equipment symbols for water treatment plants
- Clarifiers, filters, and disinfection systems
- Pumping station components
- Network distribution elements

#### Severn Trent Standards
- Sewage treatment process symbols
- Biological treatment units
- Sludge handling equipment
- Odor control systems

#### Welsh Water Standards
- Distribution network components
- Reservoir and storage symbols
- Pressure management devices
- Water quality monitoring points

#### United Utilities Standards
- Network control elements
- SCADA integration symbols
- Telemetry equipment
- Remote monitoring devices

#### Northumbrian Water Standards
- Quality monitoring symbols
- Sampling point indicators
- Laboratory equipment symbols
- Compliance monitoring devices

#### Standards Conversion
- One-click conversion between water company standards
- Mapping tables for equivalent symbols
- Automated symbol substitution
- Compliance validation for each standard

## Technical Requirements
- Multi-standard support system
- Symbol conversion engine
- Validation against water company specifications
- Import/export in water company formats

## Success Metrics
- Complete coverage of UK water company standards
- Seamless conversion between standards
- Validated compliance with each water company
- Industry acceptance and adoption
